About Me

A Career Built Thread by Thread

I am a Senior Weaving Engineer and Textile Quality Manager with over 23 years of progressive experience in weaving mills, denim manufacturers, and vertically integrated textile factories across South Asia and the Middle East.

My expertise spans the complete lifecycle of woven fabric — from yarn selection and warp preparation to loom management, fabric inspection, and export compliance. I have led quality systems implementation, driven measurable efficiency improvements, and built production planning frameworks adopted at factory-wide scale.

I specialize in bridging the gap between shop-floor operations and management reporting — combining deep technical knowledge of weaving technology with the analytical capability to manage KPIs, capacity plans, and certification programs.

🧵 Expert in rapier, air-jet, projectile & dobby loom systems
📊 Production planning for mills with 300–1000+ looms
🏅 ISO 9001 lead implementer & internal auditor
🌿 GOTS, OCS, GRS & OEKO-TEX compliance management
🔬 Uster, Zweigle & tensile testing laboratory management
